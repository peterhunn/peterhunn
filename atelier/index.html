The idea

One office for your whole life.

Atelier is a private office you don't have to run. A named manager and a small team take responsibility for the operational complexity of your life — the calendar, the household, the family, the travel, the paperwork, the appointments, the vendors, the things that keep falling to the bottom of the list — so you don't have to hold it all in your head anymore.

Work and home aren't separated, because your life isn't. Your Tuesday meeting and your child's Tuesday pickup are the same Tuesday. We manage it that way.

How it works

We ask when it matters.
Everything else gets handled.

  1. 01

    You have a named manager.

    A single person who knows how your life works, is accountable for your service, and is your point of contact. Introductions are made in person where possible.

  2. 02

    We ask only when it matters.

    Decisions that require your judgment come to you clearly and briefly. Everything else, we take care of within the boundaries we've agreed with you.

  3. 03

    One place for the whole picture.

    Your work life, your household, and your family aren't managed in separate rooms. We hold them together, because that's where the friction lives.

  4. 04

    Discretion is a matter of course.

    Your information stays yours. Your authority is documented and can be revised at any time. Everything material is auditable and available to you on request.
